Crew Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page adds up how many people a staffed post needs, how a rota lines up with the week and how leave thins a team, from the numbers you type. It does not know your labour rules, contracts, breaks, skills, sickness or who may work when, and it gives no verdict on whether any rota is legal, safe or fair. Every number is your own entry.

The cover truth

People needed to keep posts staffed, from the hours a week each person works.

The rota truth

Crews taking turns on duty in blocks of days, and how that lines up with the 7 day week.

The leave truth

What leave does to the number on duty on an average workday. Leave that bunches up makes some days worse than the average.

Crew Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
